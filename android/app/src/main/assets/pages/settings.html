Settings

Appearance

Theme
Your Android version can't set a theme per app, so Tide follows your phone's dark mode.
Reduce motion
Calmer home page and page animations.

Search

Search engine
Address bar suggestions come from your own history and bookmarks. What you type is not sent anywhere until you search.

Privacy and security

Block ads and trackers
Stops known ad and tracking servers on every site.
Remove tracking from links
Strips tags like utm_source and fbclid from addresses you open.
Block third-party cookies
Sites can't follow you across other sites with cookies.
HTTPS only
Always use the secure version of a site.
Dangerous site warnings
Warns before sites on a public malware list. Only the list is downloaded.
Google Safe Browsing
Android's built-in check for phishing and malware pages.

Clear browsing data

Tabs

Reopen tabs from last time
Private tabs are never reopened.
Move unused tabs to Orbits after
Tabs you have not looked at for this long leave your tabs and wait in Orbits. Playing and private tabs stay.
Automatic snapshots
Save your open tabs every 30 minutes and when you leave Tide.

Default browser

Make Tide your default browser
Links from other apps will open in Tide.
